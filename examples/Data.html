Celeste River

Discover the magic of Río Celeste on a guided hike through Tenorio Volcano National Park. Explore lush rainforest trails, visit the stunning turquoise waterfall, and witness the unique "Teñideros" where two rivers meet to create the river’s vibrant blue color. Along the way, enjoy the chance to spot wildlife and learn about the region’s natural wonders from an expert local guide. A perfect blend of nature, adventure, and breathtaking scenery.

Cultural Tour

Step into the cultural heart of Costa Rica with a visit to the Maleku Indigenous Village. Learn about the traditions, language, and way of life of one of the country's oldest native tribes. During this immersive experience, you’ll hear ancestral stories, see traditional dances, explore handcrafted art, and discover the deep spiritual connection the Maleku people have with nature. A meaningful and respectful cultural exchange that enriches your journey.
